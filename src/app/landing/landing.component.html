Department of Health & Family Welfare

The work you already do, finally easy to keep track of.

AshaWork helps ASHA workers log home visits, track incentives, and stay in touch with their Primary Health Centre — from a phone, even where signal comes and goes.

One dashboard

Built around your day, not around a form

Visit & task log

Record home visits, immunisation follow-ups, and referrals as you finish them — offline if you need to.

Incentive tracker

See what you've earned this month, what's approved, and what's still pending from the PHC.

Training library

Short videos and job aids in your own language, downloadable for offline viewing in the field.

Direct line to your PHC

Message your ASHA facilitator or ANM directly, without waiting for the next monthly meeting.

Getting started takes three steps

  1. 1

    Get registered

    Your ASHA facilitator adds you using your ASHA ID and phone number.

  2. 2

    Log your work daily

    Mark visits, deliveries and follow-ups as you complete them, online or off.

  3. 3

    Get paid on time

    Incentives sync to the PHC dashboard, so payments are traceable and on schedule.
